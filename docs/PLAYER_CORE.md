# Player Core V1

The Player Core is the canonical football identity layer of Atlas.

## Profile sections
- Identity: name, birth data, nationality, location.
- Football: primary/secondary position, preferred foot, competitive level, current organization.
- Physical: height, weight and other permitted football-performance fields.
- Career: organization history, teams, dates and level.
- Media: match footage, training and highlights with visibility controls.
- Documents: CV, registration, medical and identity documents with expiry/verification state.
- Development: objectives, progress and completed work.
- Assessment: Atlas Score and Scout Reports.
- Opportunities: recommended, applied and active trials.

## Profile completion
Completion is calculated from required profile fields. It is a UX indicator and must never be interpreted as player quality.

## Visibility
Player controls public/scout/authorized/private visibility where applicable. Organization access is permission-based. Minors require verified parent relationship and consent rules.

## Atlas Score
Technical 30%, Decision 20%, Physical 20%, Mentality 20%, Discipline 10%. Score and confidence are displayed separately.

## Application workflow
Applied -> Reviewed -> Shortlisted -> Invited -> Completed -> Accepted/Rejected.
Invalid transitions are rejected server-side.
